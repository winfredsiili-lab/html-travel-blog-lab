Adventures in Fire Island

My trip to Hawaii was unforgettable, from the moment I landed and felt the warm ocean air to driving up to Hawaii volcanoes National park. The park felt like another planet with black lava fields stretching for miles, steam rising from cracks in the earth and the smell of rainforest after the rain. Standing at the edge of Kilauea Caldera and watching the glow of the volcano at sunset was the highlight of the whole trip.

What makes the park so special is how much variety is packed into one place. YOu can walk through the Thurston Lava Tube, a huge tunnel formed by flowing lava of the Kilauea Iki Trail. The scenary changes every ten minutes from lush green forest to bare black rock.

The park is easy to explore in one full day but the views, the hikes and the feeling of standing on active volcanic land stay with you long after you leave.
